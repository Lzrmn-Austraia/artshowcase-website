How it works

A simple marketplace model rather than Art Showcase owning or manufacturing everything.

1

Create your store

Artists and makers join Art Showcase and receive their own storefront and profile.

2

List your products

Sell products you already make, or use any manufacturer you choose.

3

Sell across Australia

Art Showcase drives customers to the marketplace and earns a fee when a sale is made.
